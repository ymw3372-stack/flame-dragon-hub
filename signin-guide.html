How to sign in

Choose the method that matches your account. Follow the steps below and you'll be ready to explore the Hub.

Choose what you need help with

01

Sign in with email

  1. Open the Account page.
  2. Enter the email address you registered with.
  3. Enter your password and press Sign in.

You do not need a new verification email every time you sign in.

02

Create a new account

  1. Enter your email and choose a password of at least 6 characters.
  2. Press Create account.
  3. Open the verification email we send you, then return to the Account page.
  4. Press I've verified my email.

Check Spam or Junk if the email does not appear.

03

Continue with Google

  1. On the Account page, press Continue with Google.
  2. Choose the Google account you want to use.
  3. Complete Google's sign-in window.

Next time, use Google again for the same account.

04

Forgot your password?

  1. Return to the Account page.
  2. Type your account email in the email field.
  3. Press Forgot password?.
  4. Follow the password-reset email instructions.
Having trouble? Make sure the email address is correct and that you are using the same sign-in method you used when creating the account. If you still need help, open Dragon Ghost from the chat button in the corner.
